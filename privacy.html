Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This privacy policy describes how Thugnificent Workspace accesses and handles Google user data.

Who and what

Neo is a personal AI assistant operated by the account owner (published under the pseudonym “Thugnificent”) on his own computer, for his own single Google account. The app is private-use; it is not distributed or offered to others.

Data accessed

  • Gmail (read, modify, send): to triage unread mail and send/reply to messages on the owner's explicit request.
  • Calendar: to list events and create or update appointments on request.
  • Drive: to search, upload, and download files on request.
  • Contacts (read-only): to look up email addresses.
  • Sheets / Docs: to read, create, and edit documents on request.

Where data goes

Data returned through this connector is processed on the owner's own machine by the Neo assistant and shown only to the owner. Tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are not copied to any external server. There is no data sale, no advertising use, no sharing with third parties, and no use for training models outside the owner's own infrastructure.

Retention & revocation

No Google data is stored on any external server. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact: the account owner (“Thugnificent”)
