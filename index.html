Thugnificent Workspace

Thugnificent Workspace is a personal-use OAuth client that connects the account owner's Google Workspace to Neo, a self-hosted personal AI assistant running on the owner's own computer.

What it does: it lets Neo read and manage the owner's own Gmail (triage unread mail, draft/send messages on explicit request), Calendar (list events, schedule appointments), Drive (search, up/download files), Contacts (look up addresses), Google Sheets, and Google Docs — only when the owner asks Neo to do so.

Who uses it: the single account owner only. This app is not offered to the public and does not collect data from anyone else.
